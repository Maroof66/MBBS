# RESPIRATORY DISTRESS SYNDROME (RDS)

* **Definition :** Occurs in premature babies whose lungs are not fully developed.
* **Etiology :** Due to deficiency of pulmonary surfactant — **Dipalmitoyl Lecithin** (Dipalmitoylphosphatidylcholine).

---

### MECHANISM OF SURFACTANT ACTION

$$\text{Dipalmitoyl Lecithin acts as a respiratory surfactant}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Decreases surface tension of the alveolar fluid}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Keeps alveoli open \& prevents collapse of lung (atelectasis) during expiration}$$

---

### CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS (SYMPTOMS)

* a. **Rapid breathing** with grunting sound
* b. **Cyanosis** *(blue-colored lips, fingers & toes)*
* c. **Flaring of nostrils**
* d. **Subcostal / intercostal chest retractions**

---

## DIAGNOSIS

* a. **Lab / Imaging Diagnosis ($\Delta$) :—** Chest X-ray.
* b. **Prenatal Diagnosis :—**
  * Amniotic fluid analysis for **L/S Ratio (Lecithin : Sphingomyelin ratio)**.
  * **$\text{L/S Ratio} < 1 \longrightarrow$** Immature lungs (High risk of RDS).
  * **$\text{L/S Ratio} > 2 \longrightarrow$** Full-term infant (Indicates fetal lung maturity).

---

## TREATMENT (Tx)

* a. **Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP)**
* b. **Exogenous surfactant administration**
* c. **Steroids** *(antenatal corticosteroids to mother to accelerate lung maturity)* **and Antibiotics**
