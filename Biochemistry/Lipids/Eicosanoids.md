# Prostaglandins (PGs)

## Definition
- **Prostaglandins (PGs)** are **20-carbon eicosanoids** derived from **polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFA)**.
- They act as **local hormones (autacoids)** with **very short half-life (~30 sec)**.
- Present in **almost all tissues**.

> **Key Point:** PGs are **not stored**. They are synthesized **on demand** from membrane phospholipids.

---

# Classification

### Based on Ring Substituents
- PGA
- PGB
- PGD
- PGE
- PGF
- PGI (Prostacyclin)
- TXA (Thromboxane)

### Based on Number of Double Bonds

| Series | Precursor | Double Bonds |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| **Series 1** | Linoleic acid | 1 |
| **Series 2** *(Most common)* | Arachidonic acid | 2 |
| **Series 3** | Eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA) | 3 |

---

# Biosynthesis

```text
Membrane Phospholipids
        │
        │ Phospholipase A₂
        ▼
 Arachidonic Acid
        │
        │ Prostaglandin H Synthase (PGHS)
        │
        ├── Cyclooxygenase (COX)
        └── Peroxidase
        ▼
      PGG₂
        ▼
      PGH₂
        ▼
 ┌───────────────┬─────────────┬────────────┬────────────┐
 ▼               ▼             ▼            ▼
PGE₂          PGF₂α         PGI₂         TXA₂
```

> **Remember:** **PGG₂** and **PGH₂** are common intermediates.

# Regulation of Synthesis

## Stimulators

- Epinephrine
- Thrombin
- Angiotensin II
- Bradykinin
- Vasopressin
- Catecholamines → activate COX

## Inhibitors

### Steroids
⬇

Inhibit **Phospholipase A₂**

⬇

↓ Arachidonic acid release

⬇

↓ Prostaglandin synthesis

---

### NSAIDs (Aspirin)

Aspirin

⬇

Irreversibly inhibits **Cyclooxygenase (COX)**

⬇

↓ Prostaglandin synthesis

| Constitutive | Inducible |

# Mechanism of Action

```text
Prostaglandins
        │
        ▼
G-protein Coupled Receptors (GPCR)
        │
        ▼
Alter intracellular cAMP
```

- **PGE₂** → Usually ↑ cAMP
- **PGI₂** → ↑ Adenyl cyclase
- **TXA₂** → ↓ Adenyl cyclase

---

# Biological Actions

## 1. Cardiovascular System

### PGI₂ (Prostacyclin)

- Vasodilatation
- ↓ Platelet aggregation
- Protects vessel wall

### TXA₂ (Thromboxane)

- Vasoconstriction
- ↑ Platelet aggregation
- Promotes thrombus formation

> **Mnemonic:**  
> **PGI₂ = Prevents Platelets**  
> **TXA₂ = ThromboXane = Thrombus**

---

## 2. Uterus

- PGF₂α → Uterine contraction
- Medical termination of pregnancy
- Induction of labour
- Controls postpartum hemorrhage

---

## 3. Respiratory System

| PG | Action |
|----|--------|
| **PGF₂α** | Bronchoconstriction |
| **PGE₂** | Bronchodilatation |

---

## 4. Inflammation & Immunity

- ↑ Capillary permeability
- Erythema
- Wheal formation
- PGE₂ suppresses T & B cells
- PGE₂ promotes sleep

---

## 5. Gastrointestinal Tract

- ↓ Gastric acid secretion
- ↑ Intestinal motility
- Used in peptic ulcer disease
- Side effect → Diarrhea

---

## 6. Metabolic Effects

- ↓ Lipolysis
- ↑ Glycogen synthesis
- ↑ Calcium mobilization from bone

---

# Clinical Importance

### Aspirin

Aspirin

⬇

Irreversible COX inhibition

⬇

↓ Prostaglandins

⬇

- Analgesic
- Antipyretic
- Anti-inflammatory

# High-Yield One-Liners

- PGs are **local hormones (autacoids)**.
- Derived from **arachidonic acid**.
- **Not stored**; synthesized on demand.
- Half-life ≈ **30 seconds**.
- **COX** is the key enzyme.
- **Series 2 PGs** are the most abundant.
- **PGI₂:** Vasodilator + Anti-platelet.
- **TXA₂:** Vasoconstrictor + Pro-platelet.
- **Aspirin irreversibly inhibits COX.**
