# Metabolism of HDL (Exam Points)

### 1. Formation of Nascent HDL
- Intestinal cells synthesize HDL components.
- Nascent HDL is released into blood.
- It is **discoid (disc-shaped)**.

### 2. Uptake of Cholesterol
- HDL collects **free cholesterol** from peripheral tissues.
- **ABC transporter (cholesterol efflux protein)** transfers cholesterol to HDL.
- **Apo A-I** activates **LCAT (Lecithin-Cholesterol Acyl Transferase).**

### 3. Esterification of Cholesterol
- LCAT transfers a **fatty acid from lecithin** to cholesterol.
- **Cholesterol → Cholesteryl ester**.
- Cholesteryl ester moves into the **core of HDL** because it is hydrophobic.

### 4. Formation of HDL-3
- Continuous esterification fills HDL with cholesteryl esters.
- HDL changes from **disc-shaped to spherical**.
- This mature particle is called **HDL-3**.

### 5. Uptake by Liver
- HDL-3 binds to **SR-B1 (Scavenger Receptor B1)** in the liver.
- **Hepatic lipase** hydrolyses HDL phospholipids and TAG.
- Cholesteryl esters enter liver cells.
- Liver uses cholesterol to:
  - Synthesize **bile acids**, or
  - Excrete it through **bile**.

### 6. Formation of HDL-2
- If HDL-3 remains in circulation:
  - **CETP (Cholesteryl Ester Transfer Protein)** transfers cholesteryl esters from HDL to **VLDL, IDL, and LDL**.
  - In exchange, **TAG** is transferred from VLDL/IDL/LDL to HDL.
- TAG-rich spherical HDL is called **HDL-2**.
- HDL-2 is acted upon by **hepatic triglyceride lipase (HTGL)** before liver uptake.

### 7. Reverse Cholesterol Transport
- HDL transports cholesterol from peripheral tissues back to the liver.
- This process is called **reverse cholesterol transport**.
- It requires:
  - **ABC transporter**
  - **LCAT**
  - **CETP**
  - **Apo-D**

# 1-Minute Mnemonic

**"I Collect Lipoids, Make Healthy Reverse Transport."**

- **I** → Intestinal cells form HDL
- **C** → Collect cholesterol (ABC transporter)
- **L** → LCAT esterifies cholesterol
- **M** → Mature HDL-3 forms
- **H** → Hepatic SR-B1 takes HDL
- **R** → Reverse cholesterol transport (CETP → HDL-2 → Liver)
