| **Disease**                                                              | **Enzyme / Protein Defect**                                           | **Key Features**                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Hyperammonemia Type I**                                             | **Carbamoyl phosphate synthetase I (CPS-I) deficiency**               | • Very high **NH₃ (ammonia)** levels in blood  <br>• **Autosomal recessive**  <br>• Intellectual disability / developmental impairment  <br>• Incidence: ~**1 in 100,000**                                                      |
| **2. Hyperammonemia Type II**                                            | **Ornithine transcarbamylase (OTC) deficiency**                       | • High **ammonia** in blood  <br>• Increased **glutamine** in blood, CSF & urine  <br>• **Orotic aciduria** due to diversion of carbamoyl phosphate into pyrimidine synthesis  <br>• **X-linked**                               |
| **3. Hyperornithinemia–Hyperammonemia–Homocitrullinuria (HHH) Syndrome** | **Defective ornithine transporter protein (ORNT1)**                   | • Failure to transport **ornithine** from cytoplasm into mitochondria  <br>• **ORNT1 gene** defect  <br>• Hyperornithinemia + hyperammonemia + **homocitrullinuria**  <br>• Decreased blood urea  <br>• **Autosomal recessive** |
| **4. Citrullinemia Type I**                                              | **Argininosuccinate synthetase (ASS) deficiency**                     | • **Autosomal recessive**  <br>• High blood **ammonia & citrulline**  <br>• **Citrullinuria** (≈1–2 g/day)                                                                                                                      |
| **5. Citrullinemia Type II**                                             | **Defective Citrin protein**  <br>*(Aspartate–glutamate transporter)* | • Decreased availability of **aspartate** for the argininosuccinate synthetase reaction  <br>• **Fatty liver**  <br>• **Intrahepatic cholestasis**  <br>• Increased citrulline                                                  |
| **6. Argininosuccinic Aciduria**                                         | **Argininosuccinate lyase (ASL) deficiency**                          | • Increased **argininosuccinate** in blood & urine  <br>• **Friable, brittle, tufted hair**  <br>• **Trichorrhexis nodosa**  <br>• Incidence: ~**3 in 200,000**                                                                 |
| **7. Hyperargininemia**                                                  | **Arginase deficiency**                                               | • Increased **arginine** in blood & CSF  <br>• **Cysteine & lysine** lost in urine instead of arginine  <br>• Incidence: ~**1 in 100,000**                                                                                      |
