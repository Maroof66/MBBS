# 2. cAMP-Mediated Hormone Action

### Stepwise mechanism

**Hormone**
↓
**GPCR on cell membrane**
↓
**Activation of Gs protein**
↓
GDP replaced by GTP on α-subunit
↓
**Gα-GTP activates adenylate cyclase**
↓
ATP → **cAMP**
↓
**cAMP = second messenger**
↓
Activates **protein kinase A (PKA)**
↓
PKA phosphorylates target proteins
↓
**Cellular response**

### Important points

- G-protein consists of **α, β and γ subunits**.
- In the inactive state, α-subunit binds **GDP**.
- Hormone binding causes GDP → GTP exchange.
- **Gα-GTP** activates adenylate cyclase.
- Adenylate cyclase converts **ATP → cAMP**.
- cAMP binds regulatory subunits of **PKA** and releases its catalytic subunits.
- PKA phosphorylates target proteins, mainly on **serine/threonine residues**.
- Phosphorylation may **activate or inhibit** the target protein.
- This produces the final cellular response.

---

## 3. Termination of cAMP Action

- **GTPase** converts GTP → GDP → inactivates G-protein.
- **Phosphodiesterase (PDE)** converts cAMP → 5′-AMP.
- Thus, cAMP levels fall and the hormonal effect terminates.

> **Example:** Insulin activates PDE → ↓ cAMP.

---

## ⭐ Exam Flowchart

**Hormone → GPCR → Gs → Gα-GTP → Adenylate cyclase → ATP → cAMP → PKA → Phosphorylation of proteins → Cellular response**

### ⭐ Must remember

**Gs → stimulates adenylate cyclase → ↑ cAMP**

**Gi → inhibits adenylate cyclase → ↓ cAMP**

**PDE → cAMP → 5′-AMP**
