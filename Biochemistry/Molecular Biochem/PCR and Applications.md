# PCR and Applications — 5 Marks

* **1. Definition**

  * **Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)** is an **in vitro DNA amplification technique** used to produce millions of copies of a specific DNA sequence.
  * It is often described as a **“Xerox machine for gene copying.”**
  * Invented by **Kary Mullis**; Nobel Prize in 1993.

* **2. Requirements**

  * Template DNA
  * Two specific **DNA primers**
  * **Taq DNA polymerase**
  * Four **dNTPs**
  * Suitable reaction medium

* **3. Steps of PCR**

  * **A. Denaturation**

    * DNA is heated to about **95°C**.
    * Double-stranded DNA separates into two single strands.

  * **B. Annealing / Priming**

    * Temperature is lowered to about **50°C**.
    * Primers bind to their complementary sequences on the template DNA.

  * **C. Extension / Polymerization**

    * Occurs at about **72°C**.
    * **Taq polymerase** synthesizes new DNA strands using dNTPs.
    * Taq polymerase is obtained from **Thermus aquaticus** and is heat-stable.

  * **D. Repetition**

    * Denaturation → annealing → extension is repeatedly performed.
    * DNA approximately **doubles in each cycle**.
    * About **20 cycles → ~1 million-fold amplification**.

**Template DNA**
↓

↓

↓

↓
**Millions of copies of target DNA**

# Clinical Applications

* **1. Diagnosis of bacterial and viral infections**

  * Tuberculosis
  * Hepatitis C
  * CMV
  * HIV

* **2. Medicolegal / Forensic applications**

  * DNA obtained from **hair follicles, blood cells, etc.**
  * Used for **DNA identification/fingerprinting**.

* **3. Diagnosis of genetic disorders**

  * Sickle cell anaemia
  * β-thalassaemia
  * Cystic fibrosis

* **4. Prenatal diagnosis**

  * Useful when only a **small number of fetal cells** are available, e.g. after amniocentesis.

* **5. Cancer detection**

  * Detection of mutations in genes such as **p53** and retinoblastoma gene.
  * Monitoring residual abnormal cells.

* **6. Fossil/evolutionary studies**

  * DNA from fossils can be amplified and compared with DNA of living organisms to study **evolutionary relationships**.

### ⭐ Must-remember trio

**95°C → Denaturation**
**50°C → Annealing**
**72°C → Taq-mediated extension**

**definition → requirements → 3 PCR steps → amplification → 4–5 applications**.
