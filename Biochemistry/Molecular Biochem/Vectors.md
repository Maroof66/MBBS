# Vectors

* ### Definition

  * Vectors are **carrier DNA molecules** used to introduce a desired foreign gene/DNA into a host cell.
  * Most commonly used vectors are **plasmids**.

* ### Plasmid Vectors

  * Circular, **double-stranded DNA molecules** present in bacteria.
  * Can replicate independently within the bacterial cell.
  * Naturally may carry **antibiotic resistance genes**.
  * Important in the spread of antibiotic resistance among bacteria.
  * Can accommodate approximately **6–10 kbp** of foreign DNA.

* ### Bacteriophage Vectors

  * Used when larger DNA fragments need to be introduced.
  * **λ (lambda) phage** is commonly used.
  * About **40% of λ-phage genome is nonessential** and can be replaced by foreign DNA.
  * Can accommodate approximately **10–20 kbp** of foreign DNA.

* ### Cosmid Vectors

  * Hybrid vectors having features of **plasmids + λ-phage cos sites**.
  * Cos sites allow packaging of DNA into phage particles.
  * Can accommodate larger DNA fragments of approximately **20–50 kbp**.

* ### Comparison of Vectors

  | Vector                | Foreign DNA capacity |
  | --------------------- | -------------------: |
  | **Plasmid**           |             6–10 kbp |
  | **Bacteriophage (λ)** |            10–20 kbp |
  | **Cosmid**            |            20–50 kbp |

### ⭐ One-line recall

**Plasmid → 6–10 kbp → λ phage → 10–20 kbp → Cosmid → 20–50 kbp**.
