# Recombinant DNA (rDNA) Technology

### Steps involved and Applications — 10 Marks

* **1. Introduction**

  * Recombinant DNA technology is the technique of joining **DNA from two different sources** to form recombinant/chimeric DNA.
  * The recombinant DNA is introduced into a host cell for **cloning and/or expression** of the desired gene.

* **2. Preparation of Specific Gene**

  * Specific mRNA is isolated from the tissue in which the required gene is expressed.
  * **Reverse transcriptase** converts mRNA into **complementary DNA (cDNA)**.
  * cDNA represents the required coding sequence.

* **3. Selection of Vector**

  * **Vector** acts as a carrier for introducing foreign DNA into the host.
  * Common vectors:

    * **Plasmids**
    * Bacteriophages
    * Cosmids
  * Vectors contain:

    * Origin of replication
    * Selectable marker genes
    * Suitable site for insertion of foreign DNA.

* **4. Preparation of Recombinant DNA**

  * Vector DNA and foreign DNA are cut with the **same restriction endonuclease**.
  * Complementary **sticky ends** are produced.
  * Foreign DNA is annealed with the vector.
  * **DNA ligase** joins the DNA fragments by phosphodiester bonds.
  * → **Recombinant/chimeric DNA** is formed.

* **5. Introduction into Host Cell**

  * Recombinant plasmid is introduced into a suitable host, commonly **E. coli**.
  * This process is called **transfection/transformation**.

* **6. Selection of Recombinant Host**

  * Recombinant plasmids contain **selectable marker genes**, commonly antibiotic-resistance genes.
  * Antibiotic selection allows bacteria containing plasmids to survive.
  * Insertional inactivation can distinguish **recombinant from non-recombinant colonies**.
  * Selected colonies are further cultured.

* **7. Cloning / Amplification**

  * Selected host cells multiply.
  * Large numbers of identical copies of the recombinant DNA are produced.
  * → **Cloning of the desired gene**.

* **8. Expression of Desired Gene**

  * The recombinant gene may be expressed in the host cell.
  * The desired protein is produced and subsequently harvested and purified.
  * Such vectors are called **expression vectors**.

# Applications of Recombinant DNA Technology

* **1. Production of therapeutic proteins**

  * Human **insulin**
  * Human growth hormone
  * Interferons
  * Clotting factors such as **factor VIII**

* **2. Production of vaccines**

  * Recombinant vaccines, e.g. **hepatitis B vaccine**.

* **3. Diagnosis of diseases**

  * Detection of specific **genes, mutations and infectious agents**.
  * Useful in diagnosis of genetic disorders.

* **4. Gene therapy**

  * Introduction of a normal/functional gene to compensate for a defective gene.

* **5. Production of recombinant enzymes**

  * Used for production of enzymes and other biologically important proteins.

* **6. Research**

  * Gene cloning and study of **gene structure and function**.
  * Production of specific DNA sequences in large quantities.

* **7. Transgenic organisms**

  * Introduction of desired genes into organisms to obtain specific characteristics.
