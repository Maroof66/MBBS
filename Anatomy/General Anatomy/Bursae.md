# BURSAE

### Definition

* **Bursa** is a **bag-like space lined by synovial membrane and containing synovial fluid**.
* It is present in relation to **tendons, joints and bones**.
* Its main function is to **reduce friction and permit limited free movement**.
* Inflammation of a bursa is called **bursitis**.

### Types of Bursae ⭐

**1. Subcutaneous bursae**

* Present between **bony prominences and skin**.
* **Examples:**

  * Prepatellar bursa
  * Subcutaneous infrapatellar bursa

**2. Articular bursae**

* Function like a **joint**.
* **Example:** Bursa between the **dens of axis and transverse ligament of atlas**.

**3. Subtendinous bursae**
Present between:

* **Bone and tendon** → supraspinatus bursa
* **Bone and ligament** → bursa deep to tibial collateral ligament
* **Tendon and tendon** → anserine bursa

### Clinical importance

* **Bursitis** = inflammation of a bursa.
* It can cause **pain and restricted movement** due to increased friction/inflammation.

### ⭐ Quick classification

**Bursae**
→ **Subcutaneous** → bone ↔ skin
→ **Articular** → functions as joint
→ **Subtendinous** → bone ↔ tendon / bone ↔ ligament / tendon ↔ tendon
