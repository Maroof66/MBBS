# EPIPHYSIS

### Definition

* **Epiphyses** are the ends or tips of a bone that **ossify from secondary ossification centres**.

## Classification of Epiphyses

### I. According to number ⭐

**1. Simple epiphysis**

* End of a long bone develops from **multiple secondary ossification centres**.
* These centres **fuse independently with the shaft**.
* **Example:** Femur.

**2. Compound epiphysis**

* End of bone develops from **multiple secondary centres**.
* These first **unite to form a single epiphysis**.
* The compound epiphysis then fuses with the shaft.
* **Example:** Humerus.

---

### II. According to function ⭐

| Type                    | Features                                                                              | Examples                                                    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pressure epiphysis**  | Articular; participates in **weight transmission**                                    | Head of humerus, lower end of radius                        |
| **Traction epiphysis**  | Non-articular; provides **tendon attachment**; ossifies later than pressure epiphysis | Greater & lesser trochanters of femur, tubercles of humerus |
| **Atavistic epiphysis** | Phylogenetically an **independent bone** that becomes fused to another bone           | Coracoid process of scapula, os trigonum of talus           |
| **Aberrant epiphysis**  | **Not always present**                                                                | Head of 1st metacarpal, bases of other metacarpals          |

### ⭐ Compound epiphysis — important example

At the **upper end of humerus**, epiphyses of the **head, greater tubercle and lesser tubercle** unite to form a compound epiphysis, which subsequently fuses with the shaft.

### 🧠 Quick recall

**Function = P-T-A-A**

* **P**ressure → **weight**
* **T**raction → **tendon**
* **A**tavistic → **ancestral independent bone**
* **A**berrant → **absent sometimes**
