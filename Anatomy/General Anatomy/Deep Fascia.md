# DEEP FASCIA 

### Definition

* **Deep fascia** is a **tough, inelastic fibrous sheet** that invests the body deep to superficial fascia.
* It is **devoid of fat**.

### Distribution

* **Best developed:** limbs → forms tough, tight sleeves; neck → forms a collar.
* **Absent:** trunk and face.

  * Trunk → allows expansion of organs.
  * Face → allows movements of facial expression and mastication.

## ⭐ Modifications of Deep Fascia — VERY IMPORTANT

1. **Intermuscular septa**

   * Extensions of deep fascia pass between muscles.
   * Divide limbs into **functional muscular compartments**.

2. **Muscle coverings**

   * Deep fascia forms **epimysium** around whole muscle.
   * Septa extend inward as **perimysium** around muscle fascicles.
   * Fine septa form **endomysium** around individual muscle fibres.
   * These connective tissue layers carry **blood vessels, lymphatics and nerves** to muscle fibres.

3. **Nerve coverings**

   * Forms:

     * **Epineurium** → around whole nerve
     * **Perineurium** → around nerve fascicle
     * **Endoneurium** → around individual nerve fibre

4. **Sheaths around large vessels**

   * Forms vascular sheaths, e.g. **femoral sheath**.
   * Dense around artery and relatively loose around vein → allows venous distension.

5. **Retinacula**

   * Thickened bands around joints, e.g. **wrist and ankle**.
   * Hold tendons in position and act as **pulleys**, preventing loss of muscular power.

6. **Aponeuroses**

   * Modified into **palmar and plantar aponeuroses**.
   * Protect underlying nerves and vessels.
   * Also provide attachment/septa for muscles.

7. **Tendon sheaths**

   * Forms sheaths around tendons crossing joints.
   * Reduce **friction and wear** during movement.

8. **Interosseous membrane**

   * In forearm and leg, deep fascia is modified to form interosseous membrane.
   * Maintains optimum distance between bones.
   * Provides increased area for muscle attachment.
   * Transmits weight from one bone to another.

9. **Joint structures**

   * Contributes to formation of **joint capsule, synovial membrane and bursae**.

### ⭐ Special importance in lower limb

* Deep fascia forms a **tight sleeve around calf muscles**.
* Contraction of calf muscles compresses veins and lymphatics.
* Therefore, it acts as a **musculovenous/lymphatic pump**, helping venous and lymphatic return toward the heart.

### Functions

* Maintains underlying structures in position and preserves surface contour.
* Provides additional surface for muscular attachment.
* Helps **venous and lymphatic return**.
* Assists muscular action by maintaining tension/pressure around muscles.
* Retinacula act as pulleys and prevent loss of muscular power.
