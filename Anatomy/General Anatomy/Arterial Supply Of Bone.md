# BLOOD SUPPLY OF BONES

### Arterial Supply

The arterial supply of bones is derived from **nutrient, periosteal, epiphysial and metaphysial arteries**.

### 1. Long Bones ⭐

**a. Nutrient artery**

* Enters the shaft through the **nutrient foramen**.
* Passes through the cortex and divides into **ascending and descending branches**.
* These further divide into small parallel channels.
* **Supplies:** medullary cavity, **inner ⅔ of cortex and metaphysis**.
* Nutrient artery is directed **away from the growing end**.

  * **Mnemonic:** *“To the elbow I go, from the knee I flee.”*
* Clinical: emboli may lodge at the hairpin bends → **osteomyelitis**.

**b. Periosteal arteries**

* Numerous beneath **muscular and ligamentous attachments**.
* Enter the cortex through **Volkmann's canals**.
* Supply the **outer ⅓ of cortex**.

**c. Epiphysial arteries**

* Derived from **periarticular vascular arcades (circulus vasculosus)**.
* Supply the **epiphysis**.

**d. Metaphysial arteries**

* Derived from neighbouring systemic vessels.
* Enter the metaphysis directly.
* Reinforce metaphysial branches of the nutrient artery.

### 2. Long Short Bones

* Nutrient artery enters the **middle of shaft** and forms a plexus.
* Periosteal arteries supply the **major part of bone** and may replace the nutrient artery.

### 3. Short Bones

* Supplied mainly by **numerous periosteal vessels** entering through non-articular surfaces.

### 4. Vertebrae

* **Body:** supplied by anterior and posterior vessels.
* **Vertebral arch:** supplied by vessels entering at the bases of transverse processes.
* Red marrow drained by large **basivertebral veins**.

### 5. Ribs

* **Nutrient artery** enters just beyond the tubercle.
* **Periosteal arteries** also supply the rib.

---

## Venous Drainage

* Veins are **numerous and large** in cancellous bone containing red marrow.
* Example: **basivertebral veins**.
* In compact bone, veins accompany arteries through **Volkmann's canals**.

## Lymphatic Drainage

* **Lymphatics are not demonstrated within bone.**
* Some lymphatics accompany **periosteal blood vessels** and drain into regional lymph nodes.

### ⭐:

Focus heavily on **long bone arterial supply**:

**Nutrient → Periosteal → Epiphysial → Metaphysial**

And remember the key distribution:

> **Nutrient = inner ⅔ cortex + medulla + metaphysis**
> **Periosteal = outer ⅓ cortex**
