# SYNOVIAL JOINTS 

### Definition

* Synovial joints are the **most evolved and most mobile type of joints**.

### Characters ⭐

1. **Articular surfaces**

   * Covered mainly by **hyaline (articular) cartilage**.
   * In some membrane bones, fibrocartilage is present.
   * Articular cartilage is **avascular and non-nervous**.
   * Synovial fluid lubricates it → permits smooth movement.

2. **Joint cavity**

   * Present between articular surfaces.
   * Contains **synovial fluid**.
   * May be partly/completely divided by an **articular disc or meniscus**.

3. **Articular capsule**

   * Surrounds the joint and consists of:

     * **Outer fibrous capsule**
     * **Inner synovial membrane**
   * Fibrous capsule is richly innervated and detects stretching → **“watchdog action”** of the joint.
   * Fibrous capsule may be reinforced by:

     * **Capsular/true ligaments**
     * **Accessory ligaments** → intra- or extracapsular.

4. **Synovial membrane**

   * Lines the interior of the joint **except articular cartilage**.
   * Secretes **synovial fluid (synovia)**.
   * Synovial fluid:

     * Lubricates the joint.
     * Nourishes articular cartilage.
     * Viscosity is mainly due to **hyaluronic acid**.

5. **Movement**

   * Synovial joints permit **varying degrees of movement**.

---

## Classification of Synovial Joints ⭐

| Type                         | Main movement                                                     | Example                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Plane**                 | Gliding                                                           | Intercarpal, intertarsal                            |
| **2. Hinge**                 | Flexion & extension around transverse axis                        | Elbow, ankle, interphalangeal                       |
| **3. Pivot**                 | Rotation around vertical axis                                     | Superior & inferior radioulnar, median atlantoaxial |
| **4. Condylar (bicondylar)** | Mainly flexion-extension + limited rotation                       | Knee, TMJ                                           |
| **5. Ellipsoid**             | Flexion-extension + abduction-adduction; circumduction            | Atlanto-occipital, wrist, MCP                       |
| **6. Saddle**                | Flexion-extension + abduction-adduction + conjunct rotation       | **1st carpometacarpal joint**                       |
| **7. Ball-and-socket**       | Movement around multiple axes; including rotation & circumduction | Shoulder, hip                                       |
