# PROLAPSE OF UTERUS 

### Definition

* **Uterine prolapse** is the **herniation/descent of the uterus from its normal anatomical position into the vaginal canal**.

### Causes ⭐

Uterine prolapse occurs due to **weakening of the supports of the uterus**, commonly due to:

1. **Successive vaginal deliveries**
2. **Obesity**
3. **Advancing age**
4. **Connective tissue disorders**

### Signs and Symptoms

* **Heaviness/pressure** in the vagina.
* **Bulging mass/lump** into the vagina or protruding outside.
* **Painful sexual intercourse**.

### Treatment

Treatment depends on the **degree of prolapse and symptoms**:

1. **Pelvic floor exercises**

   * Strengthen pelvic floor muscles and improve support.

2. **Vaginal pessary**

   * A **silicone support device** inserted into the vagina to support the prolapsed uterus.

3. **Surgical treatment**

   * **Sacrohysteropexy:** prolapsed uterus is resuspended using a synthetic mesh attached to the **anterior longitudinal ligament over the sacral promontory**.
   * **Hysterectomy:** surgical removal of the uterus.

### 🔥 Clinical correlation

**Weak uterine supports → descent of uterus → vaginal prolapse.**
