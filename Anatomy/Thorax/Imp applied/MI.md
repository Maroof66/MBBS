## Myocardial Infarction (Heart Attack)

- A **myocardial infarction (MI)** occurs when a **branch of a coronary artery is suddenly occluded**, causing a complete loss of blood supply to a specific area of the heart.
- The affected **myocardium undergoes infarction (ischemia due to reduced blood supply)** and, if prolonged, progresses to **necrosis (tissue death)**.

### Symptoms

- **Angina pectoris** lasting **more than 30 minutes**
- **Nausea and vomiting**
- **Shortness of breath (dyspnea)**
- **Sweating (diaphoresis)**

---

## Commonly Involved Vessels in Coronary Occlusion

1. **Anterior interventricular (Left Anterior Descending, LAD) artery**
   - **40–50%** of cases

2. **Right coronary artery (RCA)**
   - **30–40%** of cases

3. **Left circumflex coronary artery (LCX)**
   - **15–20%** of cases
