# Left Coronary Artery
### Headings 
- Position 
- Course 
- Branches
- Area of Distribution

## Position 
  - Larger than RCA
  - Arises from left posterior aortic sinus
## Course

1. Origin and Initial Course
   - Runs **forwards and to the left**.
   - Emerges **between the pulmonary trunk and left auricle**.
   - Gives the **anterior interventricular (LAD) artery**.
   - Remaining continuation is called the **circumflex artery**.

2. Anterior Interventricular (LAD) Artery
   - Descends in the **anterior interventricular groove**.
   - Turns around the **inferior border of the heart**.
   - Enters the **posterior interventricular groove**.
   - Ends by **anastomosing with the posterior interventricular artery**.

3. Circumflex Artery
   - Runs to the left in the **left anterior coronary sulcus**.
   - Winds around the **left border of the heart**.
   - Continues in the **left posterior coronary sulcus**.
   - Near the posterior interventricular groove, **anastomoses with the right coronary artery**.
## Branches

   1. **Anterior Interventricular (Left Anterior Descending, LAD) Artery**

       **Branches:**
       - **Anterior ventricular branches**
         - Supply the **sternocostal surfaces** of the right and left ventricles.

       - **Diagonal artery**
         - Largest anterior ventricular branch.
         - May arise from the **junction of the LAD and left circumflex artery**.

       - **Left conus artery**
         - Supplies the **pulmonary conus**.

       - **Septal branches**
         - Supply the **anterior 2/3 of the interventricular septum**.
  
2. **Left Circumflex Coronary Artery**
**Branches:**
   - **Ventricular branches**
     - Supply the **sternocostal, left, and inferior surfaces** of the left ventricle.
   - **Atrial branches**
     - Supply the adjacent area of the **left atrium**.
   - **Sinoatrial (SA) nodal artery**
     - Arises from the left circumflex artery in **~40% of individuals**.
   - **Left marginal artery**
     - Runs along the **left border of the heart** towards the apex.
   - **Posterior interventricular artery**
     - Continuation of the left circumflex artery in **10–20% of individuals**.
   - **Kugel's artery**
     - Atrial branch of the left coronary artery.
     - Anastomoses with the corresponding branch of the **right coronary artery** along the **anterior wall of the atria**.## Area of Distribution
## Area of Distribution

1. **Left atrium**

2. **Ventricles**
   - **Left ventricle:** Greater part, except the area adjoining the **posterior interventricular groove**.
   - **Right ventricle:** Small part adjoining the **anterior interventricular groove**.

3. **Interventricular septum**
   - **Anterior 2/3** of the interventricular septum.

4. **Conducting system**
   - Part of the **left branch of the AV bundle**.
