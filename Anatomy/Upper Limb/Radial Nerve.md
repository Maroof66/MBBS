# RADIAL NERVE / MUSCULOSPIRAL NERVE

* **1. Introduction**

  * Largest branch of the **posterior cord of brachial plexus**
  * **Root value: C5, C6, C7, C8, T1**
  * Main nerve of the **extensor compartment of upper limb**

* **2. Origin**

  * Arises from the **posterior cord**
  * Given off in the **lower part of axilla**
  * Lies behind the **3rd part of axillary artery**

* **3. Course**

  * **In axilla**

    * Runs downwards behind the 3rd part of axillary artery.
  * **In arm**

    * Lies behind the **brachial artery**.
    * Passes through the **lower triangular space**.
    * Enters the **radial groove** with profunda brachii vessels.
    * Runs between the medial and lateral heads of **triceps brachii**.
    * Lies in contact with the **humerus**.
    * About **5 cm below deltoid tuberosity**, pierces the lateral intermuscular septum.
    * Enters the **anterior compartment of arm**.
  * **At elbow**

    * Descends towards the **lateral epicondyle**.
    * Enters the cubital fossa.
  * **Termination**

    * Divides just below the lateral epicondyle into:

      * **Superficial branch**
      * **Deep branch → posterior interosseous nerve**

# 4. Relations

### A. In lower axilla

* **Anterior:** 3rd part of axillary artery
* **Posterior:** Subscapularis, latissimus dorsi, teres major
* **Lateral:** Axillary nerve, coracobrachialis
* **Medial:** Axillary vein

### B. In upper arm

* Behind **brachial artery**
* Passes posterolaterally with **profunda brachii vessels**
* Passes through lower triangular space
* Between **long head of triceps and humerus**
* Enters radial groove

### C. In radial groove

* Runs downwards and laterally
* Between **medial and lateral heads of triceps**
* Closely related to **humerus**
* Accompanied by **profunda brachii vessels**
* Pierces lateral intermuscular septum
* Enters anterior compartment → cubital fossa

# 5. Branches and Distribution

### A. Muscular branches

* **Before radial groove**

  * Long head of triceps
  * Medial head of triceps

* **In radial groove**

  * Lateral head of triceps
  * Medial head of triceps
  * Anconeus

* **Below radial groove**

  * Brachialis → mainly proprioceptive fibres
  * Brachioradialis → motor
  * Extensor carpi radialis longus → motor

### B. Cutaneous branches

* **Posterior cutaneous nerve of arm**

  * Skin of posterior arm

* **Lower lateral cutaneous nerve of arm**

  * Skin of lower lateral arm

* **Posterior cutaneous nerve of forearm**

  * Skin of posterior forearm

### 🔥 High-yield course to memorize

**Posterior cord → Axilla → Behind brachial artery → Lower triangular space → Radial groove → Pierces lateral intermuscular septum → Cubital fossa → Superficial + Deep branches**


# Clinical Anatomy of Radial Nerve

* **Radial nerve injury**

  * Radial nerve is commonly injured in the **radial (spiral) groove**.

### Causes

* **Saturday night palsy**

  * Prolonged compression of radial nerve, e.g. sleeping in an armchair with the arm hanging over its side.

* **Crutch paralysis**

  * Compression of radial nerve due to improper/prolonged use of a crutch.

* **Fracture of shaft of humerus**

  * Radial nerve is closely related to the humerus in the **radial groove**, making it vulnerable to injury.

### Clinical Features

* **Wrist drop**

  * Weakness/loss of **extension at the wrist** due to paralysis of extensor muscles.
  * Patient cannot maintain the wrist in extension and therefore has difficulty in **firmly gripping objects**.

* **Sensory loss**

  * Narrow area on the **back of forearm**.
  * **Lateral side of dorsum of hand**.

### 🔥 Exam flow

**Radial nerve injury in spiral groove**
↓
**Saturday night palsy / crutch paralysis / humeral shaft fracture**
↓
**Paralysis of wrist extensors**
↓

### **WRIST DROP**

↓
**Sensory loss → posterior forearm + lateral dorsum of hand**

**Key association:**
**Radial nerve → radial groove → humeral shaft fracture → wrist drop.**
