# Cryptorchidism ⭐⭐⭐

### Definition

* **Cryptorchidism** is failure of descent of the testis, resulting in absence of **one or both testes from the scrotum**.
* It is also called **undescended testis**.

### Incidence

* Occurs in about **3% of full-term** and **30% of premature** male infants.
* In about **80%**, spontaneous descent occurs during the **first year of life**.

### Sites of undescended testis

The testis may be located:

1. **High in the scrotum**
2. At the **superficial inguinal ring**
3. Within the **inguinal canal**
4. At the **deep inguinal ring**
5. In the **iliac fossa or lumbar region**

### Clinical features / Complications

1. **Infertility**

   * Higher temperature of the abdominal/inguinal testis impairs **spermatogenesis**.

2. **Atrophy**

   * Prolonged undescended position may cause **testicular atrophy**.

3. **Malignancy**

   * Undescended testis has an increased risk of **testicular malignancy**.

### Treatment

1. **Watchful waiting**

   * Spontaneous descent commonly occurs during the **first year of life**.

2. **Orchidopexy**

   * Surgical placement and fixation of the testis in the **scrotum**.

### ⭐ One-line exam flow

**Failure of descent → testis remains outside scrotum → ↑ temperature → impaired spermatogenesis → infertility + risk of atrophy/malignancy → orchidopexy**
