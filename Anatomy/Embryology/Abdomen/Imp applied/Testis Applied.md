# Anomalies of Testis ⭐⭐⭐

### 1. [Cryptorchidism](Cryptorchidism.md)

### 1. Anorchism

* **Both testes are absent** or present only as **rudimentary gonads** in the abdomen.

### 2. Monorchism

* **Only one testis is descended** into the scrotum, while the other remains **intra-abdominal**.

### 3. Ectopic testis

* Testis deviates from its **normal pathway of descent** and comes to lie at an abnormal site.

**Sites of ectopic testis:**

* **Superficial perineal pouch**
* **In front of pubic symphysis**
* **Femoral canal**
* **Skin of penis**
* **Subcutaneous tissue in front of thigh**

### 4. Congenital hydrocoele

* Normally, the **processus vaginalis obliterates by about 1 year**.
* Persistence of part of the processus vaginalis with **accumulation of fluid** → congenital hydrocoele.

### 5. Congenital inguinal hernia

* **Persistent processus vaginalis** provides a pathway through which intestinal coils can protrude → congenital inguinal hernia.

### ⭐ Easy distinction

**Persistent processus vaginalis + intestine → congenital inguinal hernia**
**Persistent processus vaginalis + fluid → congenital hydrocoele**
