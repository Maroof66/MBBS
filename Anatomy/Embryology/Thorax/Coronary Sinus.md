# CERVICOTHORACIC VEIN — DEVELOPMENT ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The **cervicothoracic venous system** develops mainly from the **anterior cardinal veins, common cardinal veins, seventh intersegmental veins and their anastomoses**.

## Derivatives ⭐

### 1. Internal jugular vein

* From the **part of anterior cardinal vein cranial to the opening of the subclavian vein**.

### 2. Subclavian vein

* From the **7th intersegmental vein**. **[MCQ]**

### 3. Right brachiocephalic vein

* From the **right anterior cardinal vein** between:

  * Right subclavian vein
  * Oblique intercardinal anastomosis.

### 4. Left brachiocephalic vein ⭐

Develops from:

* Part of **left anterior cardinal vein** between left subclavian vein and oblique intercardinal anastomosis.
* **Oblique intercardinal anastomosis** itself.

---

# ⭐⭐⭐ 5. SUPERIOR VENA CAVA — VERY IMPORTANT

The **SVC has two developmental parts:**

### A. Extrapericardial part

* Develops from the **right anterior cardinal vein caudal to the oblique intercardinal channel**. **[MCQ]**

### B. Intrapericardial part

* Develops from the **right common cardinal vein**. **[MCQ]**

### 🧠 SVC memory

**SVC = Right anterior cardinal + Right common cardinal**

But remember the division:

**Extrapericardial → Right anterior cardinal**
**Intrapericardial → Right common cardinal**

---

### 6. Left superior intercostal vein

* A small part of the **left common cardinal vein near the oblique intercardinal channel** contributes to the left superior intercostal vein.
* Also associated with the **ligament of left vena cava**. **[MCQ]**

### 7. Oblique vein of left atrium

**(Oblique vein of Marshall)**

* Develops from the **left common cardinal vein**. **[MCQ]**

---

# ⭐⭐⭐ 8. CORONARY SINUS — VERY IMPORTANT

* Develops from the **left horn of sinus venosus**. **[MCQ]**

### 🧠 Don't mix these:

**Left horn of sinus venosus → Coronary sinus**

**Right common cardinal vein → Intrapericardial SVC**

---

# CLINICAL — ANOMALIES OF SVC ⭐⭐⭐

## 1. Double Superior Vena Cava

### Cause

* **Failure of development of the communicating channel between the two anterior cardinal veins.**
* Normally this channel develops into the **left brachiocephalic vein**.

### Result

* **Left SVC** develops from:

  * Left anterior cardinal vein
  * Left common cardinal vein
* The **left SVC drains into the coronary sinus**.

### 🧠 Flow

**No oblique intercardinal communication**
↓
**Left anterior cardinal persists**
↓
**Left SVC**
↓
**Coronary sinus**

---

## 2. Left Superior Vena Cava

### Cause

* Regression of the **caudal part of right anterior cardinal vein + right common cardinal vein**.

### Formation

* Left SVC develops from:

  * **Left anterior cardinal vein**
  * **Left common cardinal vein**

### Termination

* **Left SVC → coronary sinus**

---

## 🔥 Absolute MCQ targets

**7th intersegmental vein → Subclavian vein**

**Oblique intercardinal anastomosis → Left brachiocephalic vein**

**Right anterior cardinal → Extrapericardial SVC**

**Right common cardinal → Intrapericardial SVC**

**Left horn of sinus venosus → Coronary sinus**

**Left common cardinal → Oblique vein of Marshall**

**Double SVC → failure of oblique intercardinal communication**

**Left SVC → drains into coronary sinus**
