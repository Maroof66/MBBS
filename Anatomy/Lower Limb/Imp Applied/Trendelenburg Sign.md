# Trendelenburg Sign

### Headings

* Definition
* Abductor mechanism of hip
* Normal mechanism
* Trendelenburg test
* Positive Trendelenburg sign
* Causes
* Gait abnormalities

### 1. Definition

* **Trendelenburg sign** is positive when the **unsupported side of the pelvis drops** while the patient stands on the affected limb due to failure of the hip abductor mechanism.

### 2. Abductor Mechanism of Hip

The normal abductor mechanism consists of:

1. **Power** → gluteus medius and gluteus minimus
2. **Fulcrum** → normal relationship of head of femur with acetabulum
3. **Weight** → transmitted through head and neck of femur

### 3. Normal Mechanism

* When the body weight is supported on the **right limb**:

  * Right gluteus medius and minimus contract.
  * They elevate the **left, unsupported side of pelvis**.
  * Thus, the pelvis remains level.

### 4. Trendelenburg Test

* Ask the patient to **stand on one leg**.
* Observe the level of the pelvis.
* Normally, the pelvis remains level or the unsupported side is slightly elevated.

### 5. Positive Trendelenburg Sign ⭐

* If the abductor mechanism of the **weight-bearing limb** is defective:

  * The gluteus medius and minimus cannot adequately support the pelvis.
  * The **opposite, unsupported side of pelvis drops**.

**Example:**

> Patient stands on **right leg** → right glutei are defective → **left side of pelvis drops** → positive Trendelenburg sign.

### 6. Causes

**A. Defect of power**

* Paralysis/weakness of **gluteus medius and minimus**

**B. Defect of fulcrum**

* Congenital dislocation of hip
* Pathological dislocation of hip

**C. Defect of weight**

* Ununited fracture of neck of femur

### 7. Gait Abnormalities

* **Unilateral paralysis** of gluteus medius/minimus:

  * Patient **lurches towards the paralysed side** while walking.
  * Called **lurching gait**.

* **Bilateral paralysis**:

  * Produces **waddling gait**.

### 🧠 One-line logic to remember

**Weight-bearing side glutei hold UP the opposite pelvis.**

So:

**Weak right glutei → left pelvis DROPS → Trendelenburg +ve.**
