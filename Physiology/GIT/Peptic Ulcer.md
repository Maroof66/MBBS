# PEPTIC ULCER

* **Ulcer :** Erosion (breaking) of the surface of any organ due to shedding / sloughing of inflamed necrotic tissue that lines the organ.
* **Peptic Ulcer :** An ulcer in the wall of the **stomach or duodenum** caused by the digestive action of gastric juice.
  * In stomach $\longrightarrow$ Called **Gastric Ulcer**
  * In duodenum $\longrightarrow$ Called **Duodenal Ulcer**

---

### ETIOLOGY / CAUSES

* a. **Excessive secretion of pepsin** ($\uparrow$ increased peptic activity)
* b. **Hyperacidity** of gastric juice
* c. **$\downarrow$ Decreased alkalinity** of duodenal content
* d. **Chronic inflammation** due to ***Helicobacter pylori*** (*H. pylori*) infection
* e. **Prolonged use of NSAIDs** (Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drugs):
  * Aspirin
  * Ibuprofen
  * Naproxen
* f. **Spicy food & smoking** *(classical predisposing causes)*
* g. **Physical and emotional stress**
* h. **$\downarrow$ Decreased mucin content** ($\downarrow$ decreased mucosal protective activity)

---

### CLINICAL FEATURES

* a. **Severe burning pain in epigastric region :** Typically occurs while eating or drinking.
* b. **Nausea and vomiting**
* c. **Hematemesis :** Vomiting of blood.
* d. **Heartburn :** Burning pain in the chest due to regurgitation of acid from stomach into esophagus.
* e. **Anorexia :** Loss of appetite.
* f. **Loss of weight**

> **Clinical Feature of Duodenal Ulcer :—**  
> Pain is characteristically felt **$1 - 2\text{ hours}$ after food intake** and **during the night** *(relieved by food or antacids)*.
