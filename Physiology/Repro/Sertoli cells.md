# SERTOLI CELLS

* **Definition :** Specialized supporting cells for spermatogenic cells within the seminiferous tubules.
* **Synonyms :** Also known as **Sustentacular cells** or **Nurse cells**.
* **Morphology & Location :**
  $$\text{Large \& tall, irregular columnar cells}$$
  $$\downarrow$$
  $$\text{Extend from the basement membrane to the lumen of the seminiferous tubule}$$
* **Attachment :** Germ cells in the seminiferous tubule attach to Sertoli cells via **cytoplasmic connections**, which persist until fully mature spermatozoa are released (*spermiation*).

## FUNCTIONS OF SERTOLI CELLS

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th align="left">Function / Secretion</th>
      <th align="left">Physiological Role & Mechanism</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td><b>1. Support & Nourishment</b></td>
      <td>Provide structural support, protection, and vital nutrients to developing spermatogenic cells in the seminiferous tubules.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><b>2. Aromatase Enzyme Secretion</b></td>
      <td>Converts androgens into <b>estrogen</b>:
        $$\text{Androgens} \xrightarrow{\text{Aromatase}} \text{Estrogen}$$
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><b>3. Androgen-Binding Protein (ABP)</b></td>
      <td>Binds to testosterone, maintaining high local androgen concentration essential for testosterone activity during <b>spermatogenesis</b>.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><b>4. Estrogen-Binding Protein (EBP)</b></td>
      <td>Facilitates estrogen transport and local action within the seminiferous epithelium.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><b>5. Inhibin Secretion</b></td>
      <td>Exerts negative feedback to <b>inhibit FSH secretion</b> from the anterior pituitary gland.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><b>6. Activin Secretion</b></td>
      <td>Stimulates and <b>$\uparrow$ increases FSH release</b> from the anterior pituitary gland.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><b>7. Müllerian Regression Factor (MRF)</b><br><i>(Müllerian Inhibiting Substance / MIS)</i></td>
      <td>Secreted in fetal testes; responsible for the <b>regression of the Müllerian (paramesonephric) duct</b> during male fetal sex differentiation.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
