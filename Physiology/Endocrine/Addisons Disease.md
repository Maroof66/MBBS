# ADDISON DISEASE
### *(OR CHRONIC ADRENAL INSUFFICIENCY)*

* **Failure of adrenal cortex to secrete corticosteroids.**

---

### TYPES OF ADDISON DISEASE

* a. **Primary Addison Disease:** Due to adrenal cause.
* b. **Secondary Addison Disease:** Due to failure of anterior pituitary to secrete ACTH.
* c. **Tertiary Addison Disease:** Due to failure of hypothalamus to secrete Corticotropin-Releasing Factor (CRF).

---

### CAUSES FOR PRIMARY ADDISON DISEASE

* a. **Atrophy of adrenal cortex** due to autoimmune disease.
* b. **Destruction of gland** becoz of tuberculosis.
* c. **Destruction of hormone-secreting cells** in adrenal cortex by malignant tissues.
* d. **Congenital failure** to secrete cortisol.
* e. **Adrenalectomy** and failure to take hormone therapy.

---

### SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS

* **Symptoms becoz of deficiency of both cortisol + aldosterone.**
* **Common signs & symptoms :—**
  * a. **Pigmentation of skin and mucous membrane:**
    * $\downarrow$ *Due to excess ACTH secretion*
    * $\hookrightarrow$ Cause pigmentation by **melanocyte-stimulation action**.
  * b. **Muscular weakness**
  * c. **Dehydration** with loss of sodium.
  * d. **Hypotension**
  * e. $\downarrow$sed cardiac output and $\downarrow$sed workload of heart, **leading to $\downarrow$se in size of heart**.
  * f. **Hypoglycemia**
  * g. **Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea:**
    * $\hookrightarrow$ Cause **dehydration & loss of body weight**.
  * h. **Susceptibility to any type of infection.**
  * i. **Inability to withstand any stress**, results in **Addisonian crisis**.

---

### TEST FOR ADDISON DISEASE

* a. **Measurement of blood level** of cortisol & aldosterone.
* b. **Measurement of amount of steroids** excreted in urine.
