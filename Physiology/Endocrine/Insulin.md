# INSULIN

### SOURCE OF SECRETION
* **Secreted by B-cells / $\beta$-cells** in islets of Langerhans of pancreas.

---

### CHEMISTRY & HALF-LIFE
* **Polypeptide** ($51\text{ AA}$):
  * $\alpha$-chain: $21\text{ AA}$
  * $\beta$-chain: $30\text{ AA}$
* **Half-life:** $5\text{ minutes}$.

---

### PLASMA LEVEL
* $10\ \mu\text{U/mL}$

---

### SYNTHESIS
* **In rough endoplasmic reticulum** of $\beta$-cells in islets of Langerhans:

$$\text{Preproinsulin} \longrightarrow \text{Proinsulin} \xrightarrow{\text{Peptic cleavage}} \text{Insulin}$$

---

### METABOLISM
* **Degraded in liver and kidney** by a cellular enzyme called **insulin protease** or **insulin-degrading enzyme**.

---

### ACTIONS OF INSULIN
* Concerned with metabolism / regulation of carbohydrate metabolism and blood glucose level.

---

## 1. ON CARBOHYDRATE METABOLISM

> **Only antidiabetic hormone secreted in body $\longrightarrow$ Reduces blood glucose level by following actions:**

* a. **$\uparrow$se transport and uptake of glucose by cells:**
  * Insulin $\uparrow$ses permeability of cell membrane to glucose.
  * **Not required for glucose uptake:** Brain *(except hypothalamus)*, renal tubules, mucous membrane of intestine, RBCs.
  * **[Insulin sensitive]:** $\uparrow$se $\text{GLUT}_4$ in cell membrane (glucose transporters).
  * Glucose transported by $\text{Na}^+\text{–glucose}$ symport pump.

* b. **Promotes peripheral utilization of glucose:**
  * Rate of utilization depends upon intake of glucose.

* c. **Promotes storage of glucose (Glycogenesis):**
  $$\text{Glucose} \longrightarrow \text{Glycogen (Glycogenesis)} \longrightarrow \text{Stored in muscle \& liver}$$

* d. **Inhibits glycogenolysis:**
  * Insulin $\downarrow$es breakdown of glycogen into glucose in muscle and liver.

* e. **Inhibits gluconeogenesis:**
  * Insulin prevents gluconeogenesis *(formation of glucose from proteins by inhibiting release of AA from muscle)*.

---

## 2. ON PROTEIN METABOLISM

* **Insulin facilitates synthesis & storage of proteins** and inhibits cellular utilization of proteins by:
  * a. Facilitating transport of AA into cell from blood, by $\uparrow$sing permeability of cell membrane for AA.
  * b. Accelerating protein synthesis by influencing transcription of DNA & $\uparrow$sing translation of mRNA.
  * c. Preventing protein catabolism by $\downarrow$sing activity of cellular enzymes.
  * d. Preventing conversion of proteins into glucose.

---

## 3. ON FAT METABOLISM

* **Insulin stimulates synthesis of fat**, and also $\uparrow$ses storage of fat in adipose tissue:

* a. **Synthesis of FA and Triglycerides:**
  * Insulin promotes transport of excess glucose into cells *(liver cells)*:
    * $\downarrow$
    * Glucose utilized for synthesis of FA & triglycerides:
      * i. $\text{Glucose} \longrightarrow \text{FA}$
      * ii. $\text{FA} \longrightarrow \text{Triglycerides}$

* b. **Transport of FA into Adipose Tissue:**
  * Insulin facilitates transport of FA into adipose tissue.

* c. **Storage of Fat:**
  * Insulin promotes storage of fat in adipose tissue by inhibiting enzymes which degrade triglycerides.

---

## 4. ON GROWTH

* **Insulin promotes growth of body** by its anabolic action on proteins:
  * $\downarrow$
  * Enhances transport of AA into cells and synthesis of proteins in cells.
* **Protein-sparing effect:**
  * Causes conservation of proteins by $\uparrow$sing glucose utilization by tissues.
