# HORMONES OF ADRENAL CORTEX

* **Adrenocortical hormones are steroids in nature:**
  * $\downarrow$
  * **Hence name 'Corticosteroids'**

* **Based on their functions, 3 groups :—**
  * a. Mineralocorticoids
  * b. Glucocorticoids
  * c. Sex hormones

---

## 1. MINERALOCORTICOIDS

* **Corticosteroids that act on minerals (electrolytes)**, particularly $\text{Na}^+$ & $\text{K}^+$.
* **Mineralocorticoids are :—**
  * a. Aldosterone
  * b. 11-Deoxycorticosterone
* **Secreted by:** Zona glomerulosa of adrenal cortex.
* **Aldosterone (Life-saving hormone):**
  * Maintains osmolarity and volume of ECF.
  * $\downarrow$
  * Its absence causes **death within 3 days – 2 weeks**.

---

## 2. GLUCOCORTICOIDS

* **Glucocorticoids act mainly on glucose metabolism.**
* **These are :—**
  * a. Cortisol
  * b. Corticosterone
  * c. Cortisone
* **Secreted mainly by:** Zona fasciculata of adrenal cortex.

---

## 3. ADRENAL SEX HORMONES

* **Secreted mainly by:** Zona reticularis.
* **These are :—**
  * a. **Male sex hormones (Androgens):**
    * i. Testosterone
    * ii. Androstenedione
    * iii. Dehydroepiandrosterone
  * b. **Estrogen** $\big\}$ *(small quantity)*
  * c. **Progesterone** $\big\}$ *(small quantity)*
