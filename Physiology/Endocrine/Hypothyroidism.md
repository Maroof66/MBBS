# HYPOTHYROIDISM

* **$\downarrow$sed secretion of thyroid hormone.**
* **Hypothyroidism leads to:**
  * **Myxedema** in adults
  * **Cretinism** in children

---

## MYXEDEMA

* **Hypothyroidism in adults**, characterised by **generalised edematous appearance**.

### Causes for Myxedema:
* Due to disease of thyroid gland, genetic disorder, or iodine deficiency.
* Also due to deficiency of TSH & TRH.
* **Specific causes:**
  * a. Common cause is autoimmune disease called **Hashimoto's thyroiditis**.
  * b. In most patients, it starts with glandular inflammation called **thyroiditis** *(caused by autoimmune antibodies)*.

### Signs and Symptoms:
* **Edematous appearance throughout body:**
  * a. Swelling of face
  * b. Bagginess under eyes
  * c. **Non-pitting type of edema** *(does not make pits when pressed)*:
    * $\downarrow$
    * Becoz of accumulation of proteins with **hyaluronic acid** and **chondroitin sulfate**.
  * d. Atherosclerosis *(becoz of $\uparrow$sed plasma level of cholesterol)*
  * e. Anemia
  * f. Fatigue & muscular sluggishness
  * g. Menorrhagia & polymenorrhea
  * h. $\uparrow$sed in body weight
  * i. Scaliness of skin
  * j. Frog-like husky voice

---

## CRETINISM

* **Hypothyroidism in children**, characterised by **stunted growth**.

### Causes of Cretinism:
* Due to **congenital absence of thyroid gland**, genetic disorder, or lack of iodine in diet.

### Features of Cretinism:
* a. **Few weeks after birth:** Baby starts developing signs like sluggish movements and **croaking sound (crying)**.
* b. **Stunted growth** with bloated body.
* c. **Tongue becomes so big** that it hangs down with dripping of saliva.
* d. **Big tongue obstructs** swallowing and breathing.
* e. **Tongue produces guttural breathing** that may sometimes choke the baby.


### Cretin vs Dwarf

| CRETINISM | DWARFISM |
| :--- | :--- |
| • Mental retardation | • Development of nervous system is **normal** |
| • Different parts of body are **disproportionate** | • Parts of body are **proportionate** |
| • Reproductive function **affected** | • Reproductive function **normal** |

# GOITER

* **Enlargement of thyroid gland.**
* Occurs in both **hypothyroidism** and **hyperthyroidism**.

---

## 1. TOXIC GOITER – GOITER IN HYPERTHYROIDISM

* Enlargement of thyroid gland with **$\uparrow$sed secretion of thyroid hormones**, caused by **thyroid tumor**.

---

## 2. NON-TOXIC GOITER – GOITER IN HYPOTHYROIDISM

* Enlargement of thyroid gland **without $\uparrow$se in hormone secretion**.
* Also called **hypothyroid goiter**.

### Classification of Non-Toxic Hypothyroid Goiter:
* a. Endemic colloid goiter
* b. Idiopathic non-toxic goiter

---

### A. Endemic Colloid Goiter
* The non-toxic goiter caused by **iodine deficiency**.
* Also called **iodine deficiency goiter**:
  * $\downarrow$ *when intake $< 50\ \mu\text{g/day}$*
* **Pathophysiology:**
  * No formation of hormones *(lack of iodine)*
  * Thyroglobulin remains same *(no hormone present)*
  * $\downarrow$
  * Thyroglobulin accumulated in follicles of gland
  * $\downarrow$
  * $\uparrow$ses size of gland
* **Endemic area:** Very common in **Kashmir valley** *(soil does not contain iodine $\rightarrow$ foodstuffs do not contain $\text{I}$)*.

---

### B. Idiopathic Non-Toxic Goiter
* Goiter due to **unknown cause**.
* Enlargement of thyroid gland occurs **even without iodine deficiency**.
* **Etiology:**
  * May be due to thyroiditis and **deficiency of enzymes** such as *peroxidase*, *iodinase*, & *deiodinase* *(required for thyroid hormone synthesis)*.
  * Some foodstuffs contain goitrogenic substance:
    * $\downarrow$
    * **Goitrin** *(contains antithyroid substance like propylthiouracil)*.

---

### GOITROGENS
*(Suppress synthesis of thyroid hormones)*

* $\text{TSH secretion } \uparrow\text{ses} \longrightarrow \text{resulting in enlargement of gland}$.
* **Found in vegetables:** Turnips & cabbage *(soybean also)*.
* **Goitrogens become active only during low iodine intake.**
