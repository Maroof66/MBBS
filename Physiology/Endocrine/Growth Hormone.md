# GROWTH HORMONE

### SOURCE OF SECRETION
* GH is secreted from somatotropes [Acidophilic cell] of Anterior pituitary.

### CHEMISTRY
* GH is protein in nature.
  * Have single-chain polypeptide [191 A.A.]
  * Mw – 21,500

### BLOOD LEVEL & DAILY OUTPUT
* Blood level :—
  * Adult :— 300 ng/dL
  * Children :— 500 ng/dL
* Daily output in Adults – 0.5 – 1.0 mg

### TRANSPORT
* GH is transported in blood by – GH-binding protein (GHBPs).

### HALF LIFE
* About 20 minutes.

### METABOLISM
* Degraded in liver and kidney.

## ACTIONS OF GH

* GH is responsible for the general growth of almost all tissue of the body.
* GH ↑es the **SIZE** and **NUMBERS** of the cells by mitotic division.
* GH causes specific differentiation of specific cells:
  * Bone
  * Muscles

---

### 1. ON METABOLISM

> **GH ↑es the synthesis of proteins, mobilization of lipids, and conservation of carbohydrates.**

#### A. ON PROTEIN METABOLISM
* **GH accelerates the synthesis of proteins by :—**
  1. ↑es A.A. transports through cell membrane
  2. ↑es RNA translation
  3. ↑es transcription of DNA to RNA
  4. ↓es catabolism of proteins
  5. Promoting anabolism of proteins

#### B. ON FAT METABOLISM
* **GH mobilizes fats from adipose tissue**
  * $\downarrow$ *Leads to*
  * **↑es conc. of F.A. in the body fluids**
    * $\hookrightarrow$ *Use for production of energy [$\beta$-oxidation]*

> **NOTE :—** Due to fats mobilization:
> * $\hookrightarrow$ *Causes* production of **acetoacetic acid [ketone body]** $\rightarrow$ *Leads to* **ketosis**.
> * Also causes **fatty liver**.


#### C. ON CARBOHYDRATE METABOLISM

> **Major action of GH on carbohydrate is the conservation of glucose.**

* **Effects :—**
  1. $\downarrow$es the peripheral utilization of glucose for energy production  
     *[because Acetyl-CoA inhibits glycolytic pathway]*
  2. $\uparrow$es the deposition of glycogen in the cells.
  3. $\downarrow$es the uptake of glucose by the cells.
  4. **<u>Diabetogenic effect of GH :</u>**
     * Hypersecretion of GH $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$es blood glucose level
       * $\downarrow$ *causes*
       * $\uparrow$es insulin secretion from $\beta$-cell *(GH also stimulates directly)*
       * $\downarrow$ *due to excess secretion / activity*
       * $\beta$-cells are burnt out at one stage
       * $\downarrow$ *causes*
       * Deficiency of insulin
       * $\downarrow$ *causes*
       * **True diabetes mellitus / Full-blown diabetes mellitus**

---

### 2. ON BONE

* **GH ACTION ON BONE:**
  * **EMBRYONIC STAGE :—** Differentiation + development of bone
  * **LATER STAGE :—** Growth of skeleton
    * $\hookrightarrow$ $\uparrow$es Length + Thickness

* **IN BONE GH $\uparrow$es:**
  1. $\uparrow$es synthesis & deposition of protein by chondrocytes & osteogenic cells
  2. $\uparrow$es multiplication of chondrocytes & osteogenic cells
  3. $\uparrow$es new bone formation  
     $$\text{by converting } \text{chondrocytes} \longrightarrow \text{osteogenic cells}$$

> **$\uparrow$es the length of bone until epiphysis fuse with shaft [puberty].**
> **GH also stimulates osteoblasts strongly:**  
> $\hookrightarrow$ Bone continues to grow in thickness throughout life  
> $\hookrightarrow$ Membranous bones [Jaw, Skull bone]

---

### MODE OF ACTION OF GH - SOMATOMEDIN

* **GH acts on bone, growth & protein metabolism through <u>Somatomedin</u> secreted by Liver:**
  * $\hookrightarrow$ Polypeptide ($M_w\text{ – }7,500$)
  * $\hookrightarrow$ **Two Types:**
    * **Insulin-like growth factor-1 (IGF-I) / [Somatomedin C]**  
      $\hookrightarrow$ *Role:* Bone & Protein metabolism
    * **IGF-II**  
      $\hookrightarrow$ *Role:* Growth of fetus

* **Duration:** The action of Somatomedin C lasts for about 20 hours.
* **Mode of Action of Somatomedin C:**  
  $\hookrightarrow$ Acts through $2^{\text{nd}}$ messenger called **cAMP**.

#### GROWTH HORMONE RECEPTORS:
* **Growth Hormone Secretagogue Receptors (GHS):**
  * Transmembrane receptors
  * Belongs to cytokine receptor family
  * Receptors mainly in liver cells.

### REGULATION OF GH SECRETION

> **Hypothalamus and feedback control play important role in regulation of GH secretion.**

* **GH secretion stimulated by :—**
  1. Hypoglycemia
  2. Fasting
  3. Starvation
  4. Exercise
  5. Stress and Trauma
  6. Initial stage of sleep
* **GH secretion inhibited by :—**
  1. Hyperglycemia
  2. $\uparrow$es free F.A. in blood
  3. Later stage of sleep

---

### REGULATION OF GH SECRETION

#### 1. HYPOTHALAMUS *(by 3 hormones)*
* **Increase GH secretion:**
  * **GHRH** *(GH-Releasing Hormone)*
  * **GHRP** *(GH-Releasing Polypeptide)*
* **Decrease GH secretion:**
  * **GHIH** *(GH-Inhibitory Hormone)*

> **GHRELIN :—** Promotes secretion of GH.
