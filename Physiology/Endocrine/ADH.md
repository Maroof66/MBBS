# ANTIDIURETIC HORMONE

### SOURCE OF SECRETION
* **By supraoptic nucleus** of hypothalamus.
* **Also by paraventricular nucleus** in small quantity.
* **Transported to posterior pituitary through:**
  * $\downarrow$ Fibers of hypothalamo-hypophyseal tract
  * $\downarrow$ By means of axonic flow.

---

### CHEMISTRY AND HALF-LIFE
* **ADH is a polypeptide** ($9\text{ AA}$)
* **Half-life:** $18\text{ – }20\text{ minutes}$.

---

### ACTIONS
* **2 Actions:**
  1. Retention of water
  2. Vasopressor action

---

## 1. RETENTION OF WATER

* **$\uparrow$es facultative reabsorption of water** from DCT and collecting duct in kidney.

* **In absence of ADH:**
  * DCT and collecting duct totally impermeable to water
  * $\downarrow$
  * Reabsorption of water does not occur in renal tubules
  * $\downarrow$
  * Dilute urine is excreted
  * $\downarrow$
  * Loss of large amount of water through urine
  * $\downarrow$
  * Condition called **DIABETES INSIPIDUS**.

* **Mechanism:**
  * ADH $\uparrow$es water reabsorption in tubular epithelial membrane
  * $\downarrow$
  * By regulating water channel proteins (**Aquaporins**)
  * $\downarrow$
  * Through **$V_2$ receptors**.

---

## 2. VASOPRESSOR ACTION

* **ADH shows vasoconstrictor action:**
  * $\downarrow$
  * Causes constriction of arteries in all parts of body.
* **Due to vasoconstriction, BP $\uparrow$es.**
* **ADH acts on blood vessels through $V_{1A}$ receptors.**

> **Amount of ADH required to cause vasopressor effect**  
> $\downarrow$  
> **GREATER THAN** amount required to cause  
> $\downarrow$  
> **Antidiuretic effect.**

---

### REGULATION OF SECRETION

* **ADH secretion depends upon:** Volume of body fluid and the osmolarity of body fluids.
* **Stimulants for ADH secretion are :—**
  1. $\downarrow$se in extracellular fluid (ECF) volume
  2. $\uparrow$se in osmolar concentration in ECF

---

### ROLE OF OSMORECEPTORS

* **Osmoreceptors are receptors** which give response to change in osmolar concentration of blood.
* **Receptors situated in hypothalamus** near supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei.

#### Mechanism of Action:

$$\text{When osmolar concentration of blood } \uparrow\text{es}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Osmoreceptors activated}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Stimulate supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Send motor impulses to posterior pituitary}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Cause release of ADH}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Cause reabsorption of } \text{H}_2\text{O}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\uparrow\text{es ECF volume } \& \text{ restores normal osmolarity}$$
