# CUSHING SYNDROME

* **Disorder characterized by obesity.**
* **Hyperactivity of adrenal cortex.**

---

### CAUSES

* Due to **hypersecretion of glucocorticoids**, particularly **cortisol**.
* **Classification by origin:**
  * If due to pituitary origin $\longrightarrow$ **Cushing disease**
  * If due to adrenal origin $\longrightarrow$ **Cushing syndrome**

#### 1. Pituitary Origin
$$\uparrow\text{sed secretion of ACTH}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Hyperplasia of adrenal cortex}$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$\text{Leading to hypersecretion of glucocorticoids}$$

* **ACTH secretion $\uparrow$sed by :—**
  * a. Pituitary cells tumor, particularly in basophilic cells which secrete ACTH.
  * b. Malignant tumor of non-endocrine origin like cancer of lungs / abdominal viscera.
  * c. Hypothalamic disorder causing hypersecretion of corticotropin-releasing hormone (CRH).

#### 2. Adrenal Origin
* **Cortisol secretion $\uparrow$sed by :—**
  * a. Tumor in zona fasciculata of adrenal cortex.
  * b. Carcinoma of adrenal cortex.
  * c. Prolonged treatment of chronic inflammatory disease *(like rheumatoid arthritis)* with high dose of exogenous glucocorticoids.
  * d. Prolonged treatment with high dose of ACTH, which stimulates adrenal cortex to secrete excess glucocorticoids.

---

### CUSHING SYNDROME CLASSIFIED INTO 2 TYPES

#### 1. ACTH-Dependent Cushing Syndrome
* Due to **hypersecretion of ACTH**.

#### 2. ACTH-Independent Cushing Syndrome
* Secretion of ACTH is **normal**.
* Syndrome develops due to **abnormal membrane receptors** for some peptides like **IL-1, Gonadotropin-releasing hormone & Gastric inhibitory polypeptide (GIP)**:
  * $\downarrow$ *in cells of zona fasciculata*
  * Peptide bind with abnormal receptors
  * $\downarrow$
  * $\uparrow$ses secretion of glucocorticoids
  * $\downarrow$
  * Result in **Cushing syndrome**.

---

### SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS

* **a. Disproportionate distribution of body fat**, results in abnormal features:
  * **Moon Face:** Edematous facial appearance due to fat accumulation and retention of water & salt.
  * **Torso:** Fat accumulation in chest and abdomen; arms & legs are very slim.
  * **Buffalo Hump:** Due to fat deposit on back of neck & shoulder.
  * **Pot-Belly:** Due to fat accumulation in upper abdomen.

* **b. Purple Striae:** Reddish-purple stripes on abdomen due to:
  * Stretching of abdominal wall by excess subcutaneous fat.
  * Rupture of subdermal tissue due to stretching.
  * Deficiency of collagen fibers due to protein depletion.

* **c. Thinning of extremities**
* **d. Thinning of skin and subcutaneous tissues** due to protein depletion caused by $\uparrow$sed catabolism of proteins.
* **e. Acanthosis :—** Darkened skin patches in certain areas *(axilla, neck, groin)*.
* **f. Pigmentation of skin** *(in ACTH-dependent Cushing syndrome)*.
* **g. Facial Plethora :—** Facial redness.
* **h. Hirsutism :—** Heavy growth of body & facial hair.
* **i. Osteoporosis & bone resorption** $\longrightarrow$ Due to protein depletion.
* **j. Hyperglycemia :—** Due to gluconeogenesis $\longrightarrow$ Leads to **glycosuria & adrenal diabetes**.
* **k. Poor wound healing**
* **l. Immunosuppression** results in susceptibility for infection.
* **m. Hypertension**
* **n. Weakening of muscles** due to protein depletion.

---

### TREATMENT FOR CUSHING SYNDROME

* Include **cortisol-inhibiting drugs**, **surgical removal** of pituitary or adrenal tumor, **radiation / chemotherapy**.
