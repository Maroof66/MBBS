# HYPERACTIVITY OF ANTERIOR PITUITARY

## 1. GIGANTISM

> **Gigantism is the disorder characterized by excess growth of body before epiphysial fusion.**

### Cause :—
1. **Gigantism is due to hypersecretion of GH** in childhood or pre-adult life.
2. **Hypersecretion of GH is because of tumor** of Anterior pituitary [Acidophil cells].

---

### Signs and Symptoms :—
1. **General overgrowth of body:**
   * $\hookrightarrow$ Huge stature with height more than 7 or 8 feet.
2. **Limbs disproportionately long**
3. **Giants are hyperglycemic:**
   * $\downarrow$ *develop*
   * Glycosuria & pituitary diabetes.
4. **Constant headache**
5. **Visual disturbances:**
   * $\hookrightarrow$ Leads to **bitemporal hemianopia**.
## 2. ACROMEGALY

* **Disorder characterised by:** Enlargement, thickening, and broadening of bones, particularly in extremities of body.

---

### CAUSES
* **Due to hypersecretion of GH in adults** after fusion of epiphysis with shaft of bone.
* **Hypersecretion of GH is becoz of tumor** of acidophil cells in the anterior pituitary.

---

### SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS

  1. **Gorilla Face** $\longrightarrow$ Face with rough features:
     1. Protrusion of supraorbital ridges
     2. Broadening of nose
     3. Thickening of lips
     4. Thickening and wrinkles formation on forehead
     5. Prognathism *(protrusion of lower jaw)*
  2. **Bulldog Scalp $\longrightarrow$** Thickening of scalp, scalp thrown into folds / wrinkles.
  3. **Bitemporal Hemianopia $\longrightarrow$** Visual disturbance
  4. **Headache**
  5. **Kyphosis** *(Extreme curvature of upper back – Thoracic spine)*
  6. **Overgrowth of body hair.**
  7. **Enlargement of hands and feet.**
  8. **Enlargement of visceral organs** such as lungs, thymus, heart, liver and spleen.
  9. **Hyperactivity of thyroid, parathyroid and adrenal glands.**
  10. **Hyperglycemia and glycosuria**, resulting in **diabetes mellitus**.
  11. **Hypertension.**

---

## 3. ACROMEGALIC GIGANTISM

* **A rare disorder** with symptoms of both **gigantism** and **acromegaly**.

* **Hypersecretion of GH in children**, before fusion of epiphysis with shaft of bones:
  * $\downarrow$ *causes*
  * **Gigantism**

* **Hypersecretion of GH continued** even after fusion of epiphysis:
  * $\downarrow$
  * **Symptoms of acromegaly also appear.**

## DWARFISM

* **Pituitary disorder in children**, characterised by **stunted growth**.

---

### CAUSES

* **Reduction in GH secretion** in infancy / early childhood causes dwarfism.
  1. **Tumor of chromophobes** *(non-functioning tumor, compresses & destroys the normal cells secreting GH)*
  2. **Deficiency of GH-releasing hormone (GHRH)** secreted by hypothalamus.
  3. **Deficiency of Somatomedin C**
  4. **Atrophy or degeneration of acidophilic cells** in anterior pituitary.
  5. **Panhypopituitarism $\longrightarrow$** Reduction in secretion of all hormones of anterior pituitary gland.

---

### SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS

1. **Stunted skeletal growth** *(Height is only about 3 feet)*
2. **Head becomes slightly larger** in relation to body.
3. **Normal mental activity** with **no** mental retardation.
4. **Reproduction functions are normal.**
5. **During panhypopituitarism**, dwarfs do not attain puberty due to deficiency of gonadotropic hormones.

---

### OTHER TYPES OF DWARFISM

#### 1. LARON DWARFISM
* **Genetic disorder**
* Also called **GH-insensitivity**
  * $\downarrow$
  * Due to presence of abnormal GH secretagogue receptor in liver (GHS)
  * $\downarrow$
  * Because of mutations of genes for receptors.

#### 2. PSYCHOGENIC DWARFISM
* Child exposed to **extreme emotional deprivation or stress**
* **Short stature** *(deficiency of GH)*
* Also called **psychosocial dwarfism / stress dwarfism**.
#### 3. DWARFISM IN DYSTROPHIA ADIPOSOGENITALIS
* Also called **Fröhlich syndrome**
  * $\downarrow$
  * Pituitary disorder

#### 4. DWARFISM IN PANHYPOPITUITARISM
* **Pituitary disorder** due to reduction in secretion of **all anterior pituitary hormones**.
* **Dwarfs do not attain puberty.**
