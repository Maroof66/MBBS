# REGULATION OF ERYTHROPOIESIS

* **Factors necessary for erythropoiesis :—**
  * a. General factors
  * b. Maturation factors
  * c. Factors necessary for Hb formation

---

## 1. GENERAL FACTORS

* a. Erythropoietin
* b. Thyroxine
* c. Hemopoietic growth factors
* d. Vitamins

---

### A. Erythropoietin
* **Most important factor** for erythropoiesis.
* This is a **glycoprotein**.

* **Source of secretion :—**
  * By peritubular capillaries of kidney.
  * In small quantity from $\longrightarrow$ liver & brain.

* **Stimulant for secretion :—**  
  Hypoxia *(condition with low pressure or decreased $\text{O}_2$ availability)*.

* **Actions of erythropoietin :—**
  * a. Increase production of proerythroblasts from CFU-E of bone marrow.
  * b. Development of proerythroblasts into matured RBCs.
  * c. Increase release of matured erythrocytes into blood.

> **Blood level of erythropoietin increases in anemia.**

---

### B. Thyroxine
* Thyroxine accelerates process of erythropoiesis at many levels.

---

### C. Hemopoietic Growth Factors
* These are **interleukins & stem cell factor** which increase RBC formation.
* **Interleukins involved :—**
  * a. **Interleukin-3 (IL-3):** Secreted by T-cells.
  * b. **IL-6:** By T-cells, macrophages, endothelial cells.
  * c. **IL-11:** By osteoblasts.

---

### D. Vitamins

* a. **Vitamin B :—**  
  Its deficiency causes **anemia & pellagra** *(disease characterized by skin lesions, diarrhea, weakness, nervousness, dementia)*.

* b. **Vitamin C :—**  
  Deficiency causes **anemia + scurvy** *(disease characterized by impaired collagen synthesis, rough skin, bleeding gum, loosening of teeth, bone pain, poor wound healing, lethargy, emotional changes)*.

* c. **Vitamin D :—** Deficiency causes **anemia + rickets**.
* d. **Vitamin E :—** Deficiency causes **anemia + malnutrition**.

---

## 2. MATURATION FACTORS

* a. Vit. $\text{B}_{12}$
* b. Intrinsic factor of Castle
* c. Folic acid

---

### A. Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$ (Cyanocobalamin / Anti-Pernicious Factor)

* **Source :—**
  * Vit. $\text{B}_{12}$ called **extrinsic factor**.
  * From diet.
  * Stored mainly in liver.
  * Also produced in large intestine by intestinal flora.

* **Action :—**
  * Essential for **synthesis of DNA** in RBCs.
  * **Deficiency leads to :—**
    * Failure in maturation of cells.
    * Reduction in cell division.
    * Cells are larger with fragile & weak cell membrane $\longrightarrow$ **Macrocytic anemia**.
    * Causes **pernicious anemia**.

---

### B. Intrinsic Factor of Castle

* Produced in gastric mucosa by **parietal cells of gastric glands**.
* Essential for **absorption of Vit. $\text{B}_{12}$** from intestine.

> **Hematinic Principle :—**  
> Produced by action of intrinsic factor on extrinsic factor *(also called **antianemia principle**)*.

---

### C. Folic Acid

* Required for **synthesis of DNA**.
* Absence of folic acid $\longrightarrow$ causing failure of maturation $\longrightarrow$ **leads to anemia**.
* *(Cells are larger & appear in megaloblastic stage $\longrightarrow$ **Megaloblastic anemia**)*.

---

## 3. FACTORS NECESSARY FOR Hb FORMATION

* a. **1ˢᵗ class proteins & amino acids :—** Synthesis protein part of Hb $\longrightarrow$ **Globin**.
* b. **Iron :—** Necessary for formation of **heme part** of Hb.
* c. **Copper :—** Necessary for **absorption of iron** from GI tract.
* d. **Cobalt and Nickel :—** Essential for **utilization of iron** during Hb formation.
* e. **Vitamins :—** Vit. C, riboflavin, nicotinic acid & pyridoxine.
