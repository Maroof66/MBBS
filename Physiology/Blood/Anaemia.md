# ANEMIA

* **Definition:** Anemia is a blood disorder, characterised by reduction in:
  * a. Red blood cell (RBC) count
  * b. Hemoglobin (Hb) content
  * c. Packed cell volume (PCV)

> **Because of :—**  
> • Decreased production of RBC  
> • Increased destruction of RBC  
> • Excess loss of blood from body

---

## CLASSIFICATION OF ANEMIA

* Classified by 2 methods:
  * 1. Morphological classification
  * 2. Etiological classification

---

### 1. MORPHOLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION

* Depends upon:
  * **Size of RBC $\longrightarrow$** Determined by MCV *(Mean Corpuscular Volume)*
  * **Color of RBC $\longrightarrow$** Determined by MCHC *(Mean Corpuscular Hb Concentration)*

* **Types :—**
  * a. **Normocytic Normochromic Anemia:** Size & color are normal; number of RBCs is less.
  * b. **Macrocytic Normochromic Anemia:** Size is large; color is normal; RBC count is less.
  * c. **Macrocytic Hypochromic Anemia:** Size is large; MCHC is low (pale colour).
  * d. **Microcytic Hypochromic Anemia:** Small size + less color.

---

### 2. ETIOLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION
*(Study of cause or origin)*

* a. Hemorrhagic anemia
* b. Hemolytic anemia
* c. Nutrition deficiency anemia
* d. Aplastic anemia
* e. Anemia of chronic disease

---

#### A. Hemorrhagic Anemia
* Anemia due to **excessive loss of blood**.

* **Types :—**
  * **Acute Hemorrhage:**
    * Loss of large quantity of blood as in case of accident.
    * Decreased RBC count causes hypoxia $\longrightarrow$ corrected within 4 – 6 weeks.
    * **RBCs are:** Normocytic, normochromic.
  * **Chronic Hemorrhage:**
    * Loss of blood by internal / external bleeding over a long period of time *(e.g., peptic ulcer, menorrhagia)*.
    * **RBCs are:** Microcytic, hypochromic.
    * Replacement of RBCs does not occur quickly.

---

#### B. Hemolytic Anemia
* Anemia due to **excessive destruction of RBCs** *(not compensated by increased RBC production)*.

* **Types :—**
  * **Extrinsic Hemolytic Anemia:**
    * Caused by destruction of RBCs by external factors *(e.g., hypersplenism, liver failure, renal disorder)*.
  * **Intrinsic Hemolytic Anemia:**
    * Caused by destruction of RBCs becoz of defective RBCs *(e.g., sickle cell anemia, thalassemia)*.

---

#### C. Nutrition Deficiency Anemia
* Anemia occurs due to deficiency of a nutritive substance necessary for erythropoiesis.

* **Types :—**
  * **Iron (Fe) Deficiency Anemia:** RBCs are microcytic & hypochromic.
  * **Vitamin B12 Deficiency (Pernicious Anemia / Addison's Anemia):** RBCs are macrocytic & normochromic / hypochromic.
  * **Folic Acid Deficiency (Megaloblastic Anemia):** RBCs are megaloblastic & hypochromic.

---

#### D. Aplastic Anemia
* Anemia due to disorder of red bone marrow.
* Red bone marrow reduced & replaced by fatty tissues.
* **Causes:** Bone marrow disorder occurs in repeated exposure to X-ray, tuberculosis, viral infections *(hepatitis)*.
* **RBCs are:** Normocytic & normochromic.

---

#### E. Anemia of Chronic Diseases
* Anemia develops after few months of sustained disease.
* **RBCs are:** Normocytic & normochromic.
* **Occurs in chronic infections :—**
  * Tuberculosis
  * Chronic renal failure
  * Cancer disorders
  * Rheumatoid arthritis

---

## SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS OF ANEMIA

* a. **Skin and Mucous Membrane $\longrightarrow$** Color of skin and mucous membrane becomes pale.
* b. **Cardiovascular System $\longrightarrow$** Increase in HR & cardiac output.
* c. **Respiration $\longrightarrow$** Increase in rate & force of respiration *(sometimes leads to breathlessness, dyspnea)*.
* d. **Digestion $\longrightarrow$** Anorexia, nausea, vomiting, abdominal discomfort.
* e. **Kidney $\longrightarrow$** Renal function disturbed.
* f. **Reproductive System $\longrightarrow$** In female, menstrual cycle disturbed.
* g. **Neuromuscular System $\longrightarrow$** Headache, lack of concentration, restlessness, irritability, vertigo.
* h. **Muscles $\longrightarrow$** Muscles become weak & patient feels lack of energy and fatigued quite often & quite easily.
