# GLOMERULAR FILTRATION RATE [GFR]

* **Definition :** Total quantity of filtrate formed in all the nephrons of both the kidneys in a given unit of time.
* **Normal Value :** **$125\text{ mL/minute}$** or **$180\text{ L/day}$**.
* **Composition of Glomerular Filtrate :** Same constituents as blood plasma, except glomerular filtrate **lacks plasma proteins and blood cells**.

---

## MATHEMATICAL FORMULA & STARLING FORCES

$$\text{GFR} = K_F \Big[ (P_{GC} - P_{BS}) - (\pi_{GC} - \pi_{BS}) \Big]$$

* **$K_F$ (Filtration Coefficient) :** Membrane permeability and surface area product ($= 12.5\text{ mL/min/mmHg}$).
* **$P_{GC}$ (Glomerular Capillary Hydrostatic Pressure) :** $= 40\text{ mmHg}$
* **$P_{BS}$ (Bowman's Capsule Hydrostatic Pressure) :** $= 10\text{ mmHg}$
* **$\pi_{GC}$ (Glomerular Capillary Colloid Osmotic / Oncotic Pressure) :** $= 20\text{ mmHg}$
* **$\pi_{BS}$ (Bowman's Capsule Colloid Osmotic Pressure) :** $= 0\text{ mmHg}$

---

## STEP-BY-STEP CALCULATION

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Net Filtration Pressure (NFP)} &= (P_{GC} - P_{BS}) - (\pi_{GC} - \pi_{BS}) \\
&= (40 - 10) - (20 - 0) \\
&= 30 - 20 \\
&= 10\text{ mmHg}
\end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{GFR} &= K_F \times \text{NFP} \\
&= 12.5 \times 10 \\
&= \mathbf{125\text{ mL/min}}
\end{aligned}$$

# RENAL PHYSIOLOGY FORMULAS & CALCULATIONS

---

### 1. Filtration Fraction (FF)

$$\text{Filtration Fraction} = \frac{\text{GFR}}{\text{Renal Plasma Flow (RPF)}} \times 100$$

* **Normal Value :** **$19.2\%$** *(approx. $20\%$)*

---

### 2. Net Filtration Pressure (NFP)

$$\text{Net Filtration Pressure} = \text{Glomerular Capillary Pressure} - (\text{Colloid Osmotic Pressure} + \text{Hydrostatic Pressure in Bowman's Capsule})$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{NFP} &= 60 - (25 + 15) \\
&= 60 - 40 \\
&= \mathbf{20\text{ mmHg}}
\end{aligned}$$

---

### 3. Filtration Coefficient ($K_F$)

$$\text{Filtration Coefficient } (K_F) = \frac{\text{GFR}}{\text{Net Filtration Pressure}}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
K_F &= \frac{125\text{ mL/min}}{20\text{ mmHg}} \\
&= \mathbf{6.25\text{ mL/min/mmHg}}
\end{aligned}$$
